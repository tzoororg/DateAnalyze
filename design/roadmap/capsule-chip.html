The roadmap premise doesn't hold up against the CSS:

  • It isn't the only olive/gold element. --butter-bg/--butter-fg is a first-class token used by .chip.butter, .tier-pill, .sticker-tag.butter, .tag.explore and .mood-tag. The capsule chip is the standard butter chip (same vars, same pill recipe). Restyling it to "standard" (option b) would just move it from the butter family to the pink family.
  • The label isn't dark-on-olive or borderline. It's light gold #eec97a on dark olive #4a3d22 — 6.70:1, comfortably past WCAG AA (4.5:1) and past AAA for this bold 12px weight-700 text by the large-text rule's spirit; it also beats option (b)'s 6.50:1.
  • Option (a) buys 9.67:1 nobody asked for at the cost of a near-invisible chip edge (1.03:1 vs card) and a fourth gold shade in the system.

The genuinely borderline chips are the Twilight vibe-suggestion chips — already a separate medium backlog item; fix contrast there, not here.
